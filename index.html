Data, calculation and scope

Annual academic-year costs for new 2026–27 undergraduates, generally excluding summer. This is not a 2027–28 quote. Costs are campus budgets, not major-specific tuition quotes. Housing, insurance, cohort and enrollment can change actual costs.

Gift aid = grants + scholarships. Net cost = max(0, expenses − gift aid). Total financial aid = gift aid + loans + work-study. Remaining gap = max(0, net cost − loans − work-study − personal/family contribution). Work-study is a potential earnings plan, not an upfront bill credit.

Subject filters organize funding interests; they do not assert that every campus offers an identically named major. AI-related opportunities include computing and data science. UCSF is excluded because this dashboard covers bachelor’s enrollment. Scholarships are selective and may not be stackable. Blue and Gold is not counted as a separate award.

Sources checked October 5, 2026 (Pacific). Scholarship cycle status appears in each card where known. Editable fields are temporary and reset when the page reloads.
